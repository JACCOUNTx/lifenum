訂閱與收費

  • iOS 版訂閱由 Apple App Store 處理：開通後可於「設定 → Apple ID → 訂閱」查看、更改或取消。
  • 取消後仍可使用至當期結束，不會立即扣款或中斷。
  • 如已購買但未開通會員，請在 App 內「我的 → 恢復購買」重新同步。
  • Android 版訂閱由 Google Play 處理：開啟 Google Play → 付款與訂閱 → 訂閱項目。

帳戶與資料

  • 可在 App 內「我的 → 刪除帳戶」即時永久刪除帳戶、姓名與出生日期。
  • 未登入時，個人資料只儲存在你的裝置上。

Support

LifeNum

Need help? Email info@marlleegroup.com and we will usually reply within two business days.

Subscriptions

  • iOS subscriptions are handled by Apple: manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions after purchase.
  • If your membership is missing after purchase, open My → Restore purchases in the app.
  • Android subscriptions are handled by Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Account

  • You can permanently delete your account in the app under My → Delete account.
  • Without signing in, your profile stays on this device only.
