刪除帳戶

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 21 日 · 聯絡：info@marlleegroup.com

你可以隨時刪除「命數五行穿衣法」帳戶。刪除後會同時取消有效訂閱，並永久移除伺服器上的帳戶、姓名、出生日期及會籍資料，無法復原。

方法一：在應用程式內刪除（即時）

  1. 登入應用程式或網站 https://lifenum.pages.dev。
  2. 前往「我的」頁面。
  3. 在「會員帳戶」卡片底部按「刪除帳戶」。
  4. 輸入你的登入密碼，按「確認刪除」。

如你仍有有效訂閱，系統會先即時取消 Stripe 訂閱，再刪除帳戶。已收取的費用一般不會按比例退款，除非法律要求或另行說明。

方法二：電郵申請

如你無法登入，請以註冊時的電子郵件寄至 info@marlleegroup.com，主旨註明「刪除帳戶申請」。我們會在核實身份後 30 天內完成刪除。

本機資料

穿搭記錄與語言設定只保存在你的裝置。刪除帳戶後，可在應用程式「我的 → 清空所有資料」清除，或在瀏覽器清除該網站的資料。

需要保留的資料

基於稅務、會計或法律要求，Stripe 可能保留已完成的付款與發票記錄；該等記錄由 Stripe 按其政策管理。

Delete your account

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Contact: info@marlleegroup.com

You can delete your LifeNum account at any time. Deletion cancels any active subscription and permanently removes your account, name, birth date and membership data from our servers.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Sign in to the app or https://lifenum.pages.dev.
  2. Open the “My” tab.
  3. At the bottom of the account card, tap “Delete account”.
  4. Enter your password and confirm.

If you have an active subscription, we cancel the Stripe subscription immediately before deleting the account. Amounts already charged are generally non-refundable unless required by law.

Option 2: Email request

If you cannot sign in, email info@marlleegroup.com from your registered address with the subject “Account deletion request”. We will verify and complete the deletion within 30 days.

On-device data

Your outfit journal and language setting stay on your device. After deleting your account, use “My → Clear all data” in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser.

Data we must retain

Stripe may retain completed payment and invoice records for tax, accounting or legal reasons under its own policies.
